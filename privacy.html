Better Dashboard for Canvas: Privacy Policy

Effective Nov 5, 2026

Summary

Better Dashboard for Canvas keeps your data on your own device. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information, and it has no analytics, tracking or ads. The only outside service it talks to, apart from your school's Canvas, is ExtensionPay, and only to check whether you've bought the premium themes.

Information from Canvas

The extension reads your Canvas data using the Canvas session you're already logged into. It never asks for or stores your Canvas password or an access token, and it only makes read requests.

It reads:

  • Your courses, assignments, quizzes, due dates and calendar events
  • Your grades and assignment scores, to show grades and what-if calculations
  • Course announcements
  • Your school's Canvas address, so it knows where to connect

All of this is stored in your browser's local extension storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any server we run. We do not run any servers.

The extension also stores things you create in it: your theme and other settings, checked-off assignments, custom calendar events, what-if grades and study-timer history. These stay on your device too.

To theme Canvas pages, the extension reads Canvas's own style files from Canvas and its content delivery network. Those files contain only page styling, never your personal data.

Payments

Premium themes are a one-time purchase handled by ExtensionPay, which processes payments through Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

When you buy or restore a purchase, ExtensionPay and Stripe collect the information needed for the payment, such as your email address and payment details. The extension itself only receives whether you have paid. ExtensionPay keeps an identifier for your installation so it can confirm your purchase and let you restore it on another browser.

Your payment information is governed by ExtensionPay's and Stripe's own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We don't share your data with anyone, apart from the payment step above.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, cookies or ads.
  • We don't use your data for anything other than running the extension's features.
  • We don't load code from remote servers. All of the extension's code ships inside it.

The extension's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset everything to clear your data from the extension, or uninstall it to remove all of its local data. To have your purchase record removed from ExtensionPay, contact us at the address below.

Children

The extension is made for college students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
